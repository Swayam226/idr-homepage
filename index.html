About IDR

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) is an industry-led training and deployment institute for digital, cyber and technology risk roles—built for regulated, high‑consequence environments where resilience and accountability are non‑negotiable.

We combine UK university partnership and applied research with real delivery: learners build practical capability through projects that reflect modern risk, assurance and AI governance needs.

Academic insight, deployed.

IDR brings together university collaboration, practitioners, and employers to create a repeatable pathway from training to outcomes—so organisations can adopt frameworks and deliver risk work with confidence.

  • Curriculum aligned to recognised standards and regulation
  • Portfolio-based assessment and evidence of capability
  • Deployment-ready talent for delivery teams

Our model

A simple, repeatable approach that trains people, supports employers, and advances next‑generation digital and AI risk capability.

Academy

Bootcamps and structured pathways for students, early‑career hires, and professionals.

  • Digital risk foundations
  • Cyber assurance & control testing
  • AI risk and governance essentials

Innovation & Incubation

Applied research and IP development for emerging threat and model‑risk challenges.

  • Future risk models
  • AI governance playbooks
  • Tooling and assurance methods

Advisory Services

Practical support to implement risk frameworks and improve operational resilience.

  • NIST CSF / 800-series alignment
  • ISO 27001 program support
  • NIS2 readiness and evidence
